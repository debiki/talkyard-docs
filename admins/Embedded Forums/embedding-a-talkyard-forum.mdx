Embedding a Talkyard forum
==========================

You can embed a Talkyard forum in an `<iframe>` on your website.

In most cases you wouldn't want to do that — it's better for SEO to have your
forum on its own subdomain instead of in an iframe. But there are exceptions.

## Step-by-step instructions

Create a Talkyard forum. Let's say its address is: `https://talkyard.example.com`,
and your website is at: `https://www.example.com`.

Then, to embed the forum, first configure Talkyard, then your website:


### Configuring Talkyard

1. Go here: `https://talkyard.example.com/-/admin/settings/embedded-comments`

1. On that page, in the **Allow embedding from** field, type the address to your website,
   that is, `http://www.example.com` in our example.

1. Ignore the "_Which blog do you use?_" and "_Instructions for ..._" section
   — they're for blog comments.

1. Click **Save all changes** at the bottom.


### Configuring your website

Over at your website, on the page where you want to embed the forum:

1. Add a variable, `talkyardServerUrl`, with the URL to your Talkyard forum,
    and also load Talkyard's javascript:

    ```
    <script type="text/javascript">
    talkyardServerUrl = 'https://talkyard.example.com';
    </script>
    <script async defer src="https://talkyard.example.com/-/talkyard-forum.min.js"></script>
    ```

2. Add this `<div>` where you want the forum to appear:

    ```
    <div class="talkyard-forum"></div>
    ```

3. Reloade the page, and the forum should appear in an `<iframe>`.

Exactly how you make the above changes, depends on what website software you use.
For example, if you have a WordPress website, or a Hugo static website,
or something else. You might need to consult the documentation.

Ask for help in our forum: https://forum.talkyard.io


## Deep links

Deep links into the iframe with the embedded forum, should just work:
The `talkyard-forum.min.js` script on [the page that embeds the forum]
automatically updates the URL, so the #hash-fragment points to
the page shown in the forum `<iframe>`.

For example, if your website and forum page is: `https://www.example.com/forum`,
and the URL path to the Talkyard page shown in the `<iframe>` is: `/-123/talkyard-page`,
then Talkyard would update the web browser's URL to:
`https://www.example.com/forum#/-123/talkyard-page` (note the `#` in the middle).

And if you paste this the above URL into the browser address bar,
Talkyard will look at the URL #hash-fragment, that is, `#/-123/talkyard-page`,
and show page `/-123/talkyard-page` in the `<iframe>`.
